# 🚨 Fraud Intelligence & Risk Analytics Dashboard

An enterprise-grade Power BI solution designed to provide a 360° view of Fraud, Investigations, Financial Impact, Recovery Performance, and Risk Exposure across the organization.

The dashboard leverages advanced Power BI capabilities such as **Field Parameters, Bookmarks, Tooltips, Drillthrough Navigation, Dynamic Insights Panels, Pareto Analysis, Waterfall Analysis, and Interactive Storytelling** to deliver actionable business insights.

---

## 📊 Dataset Overview

- **5,000+ Fraud Investigation Records**
- **Jan 2022 – Jun 2026**
- **57+ Attributes**
- Fraud Cases, Investigators, Branches, Customers, Financial Losses, Recoveries, Risk Scores, Compliance & TAT Metrics

---

# 📂 Dashboard Pages

## 1️⃣ Executive Command Center

A high-level executive overview of fraud operations and business impact.

### Key Features
- Executive KPI Scorecards
- Dynamic Fraud Explorer
- Trend Intelligence
- Executive Insights Panel
- Interactive Tooltips
- Dynamic Measure & Dimension Switching

### Business Questions Answered
- What is happening across the fraud landscape?
- How many active and closed cases exist?
- Which fraud categories dominate?
- Is fraud exposure increasing or decreasing?

---

## 2️⃣ Investigation Operations Center

A dedicated operational intelligence page focused on investigation efficiency and compliance.

### Views

#### Backlog View
- Aging Analysis
- Backlog Heatmaps
- Open Case Monitoring
- Branch Backlog Tracking

#### Investigator View
- Investigator Workload Analysis
- Performance Matrix
- Resolution & Closure Trends
- Team Productivity Analysis

#### Compliance View
- TAT/SLA Monitoring
- Escalation Analysis
- Branch Compliance Performance
- Compliance Trend Tracking

### Business Questions Answered
- Where are investigations getting delayed?
- Which investigators are overloaded?
- Which branches are failing compliance targets?
- How effectively are teams closing cases?

---

## 3️⃣ Loss & Recovery Intelligence

A financial intelligence page focused on exposure, recovery performance, and loss drivers.

### Views

#### Exposure View
- Financial Exposure Explorer
- Exposure Concentration Analysis
- Exposure Trend Monitoring
- Exposure Risk Distribution

#### Recovery View
- Recovery Performance Explorer
- Recovery Efficiency Analysis
- Recovery Trend Monitoring
- Recovery Health Indicators

#### Loss Drivers View
- Net Loss Driver Explorer
- Financial Waterfall Analysis
- Pareto Analysis (80/20 Loss Drivers)
- Loss Concentration Monitoring

### Business Questions Answered
- Where is financial exposure concentrated?
- How effectively are losses being recovered?
- Which fraud drivers contribute most to financial impact?

---

## 4️⃣ Risk Intelligence Hub

A comprehensive risk-monitoring page focused on identifying high-risk entities, fraud exposure, and emerging threats.

### Key Features
- Risk Score Intelligence
- Severity Distribution Analysis
- Branch Risk Matrix
- Customer Risk Segmentation
- VIP Risk Monitoring
- Fraud Risk Heatmaps
- High-Risk Case Analysis
- Emerging Risk Trend Tracking

### Business Questions Answered
- Which branches carry the highest fraud risk?
- Which customer segments require attention?
- Where are high-risk cases concentrated?
- How is risk evolving over time?

---

## 5️⃣ Case Explorer

A detailed investigation workspace designed for deep case-level analysis via drillthrough navigation.

### Key Features

- Case Timeline Analysis
- Investigator & Team Details
- Financial Impact Breakdown
- Risk Assessment Summary
- Recovery Tracking
- Root Cause Analysis
